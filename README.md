# Alliouagana Arc

A website for **Alliouagana Arc – UKOT CIC**, a community interest company focused on supporting Caribbean UK Overseas Territories (UKOT) citizens who are navigating the United Kingdom National Health Service (NHS) and related non-clinical challenges.

## About the Project

Alliouagana Arc is a one-page, responsive website that introduces the organization, explains the need for its services, and outlines the support it offers. It is designed to communicate the mission of the CIC clearly and compassionately to visitors, partners, and community members.

The site highlights:

- Who Alliouagana Arc is
- The challenges faced by UKOT citizens using the NHS referral system
- The organization’s services and activities
- Community values and guiding principles
- Contact information and social links
- A survey modal for community feedback and data collection

## Features

- Clean, modern one-page landing page
- Responsive layout for desktop and mobile devices
- Smooth scrolling navigation
- Hero section with mission statement
- Sections for:
  - Who We Are
  - Statement of Need
  - Our Activities & Services
  - Values
  - Contact details
- Survey modal with links to community surveys
- Tailwind CSS styling via CDN
- Google Fonts and Font Awesome integration

## Tech Stack

- HTML5
